Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. Party Hearty is made by Tai Maor.

Party Hearty doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and the app makes no connections to servers of ours.

What stays on your Mac

Your settings, your pet's name, your visitor collection and any stickers you add are saved on your Mac only. Window positions are read on your Mac to place the toys; Party Hearty never reads window titles or contents, and never stores or sends the positions anywhere. So that pets can walk along your Dock, it reads the Dock's icon size and how many items it has, on your Mac, without changing anything.

Weather sync

Weather sync is off until you turn it on. When it's on, Party Hearty sends the place you typed (a city or ZIP code) to Apple to find it on the map, or, if you choose "Use my Mac's location", your Mac's approximate location (about 3 km accuracy) to Apple Weather, and asks for the current weather there about every 30 minutes. That request goes straight from your Mac to Apple; it never reaches us. Apple says WeatherKit uses location only to provide the forecast and doesn't associate it with your identity. The last result (the weather and the place) is kept only on your Mac so the app can show it after a restart. Turn weather sync off and nothing is sent.

Microphone and Screen Recording

Both are optional and only asked for when you turn on the feature that needs them: Dance to Music listens for the beat, and Retro Wrecker takes one still picture of your screen to smash. Neither ever leaves your Mac, and nothing is recorded or saved.

Purchases

Party Hearty is bought through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; we never see your name, email or payment details.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its data from your Mac.

Children

Party Hearty doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated. If Party Hearty ever starts collecting anything, the app will say so first.
