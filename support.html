Common questions

Where is Party Hearty? In your menu bar, as a little app window wearing a party hat. It has no Dock icon. Click the hat to turn toys on and off, or to open Settings.

How do I hide everything quickly? Press Control-Option-Command-P. Press it again to bring the party back. You can also right-click any toy and choose Hide Everything.

Can I click through the toys? Yes. Party Hearty only takes a click right on a toy, so everything else on your screen works as usual. Drag a pet to pick it up and throw it; right-click it to feed it, pet it or send it for a nap.

Toys disappear when I watch a video full screen. That's on purpose: they step aside for full-screen apps and come back when you leave. Quiet hours in Settings can also hide them on a schedule.

The weather doesn't sync. Check that Settings › Weather › "Sync with the weather outside" is on and that you're online. If you chose "Use my Mac's location", allow Party Hearty in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, or type a city instead.

Does it use a lot of battery? No. When nothing moves it draws almost nothing, and on battery or in Low Power Mode it slows down by itself. Settings › General › Performance lets you choose Least Energy.

How do I start it when I log in? Settings › General › "Open at login".
