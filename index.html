Party Hearty

Desktop pets, weather and little toys that live on your Mac's desktop, around your windows.

A pet naps on your Dock and wanders along the tops of your windows. Rain trickles down their sides, snow piles up on them, and visitors drop by to be collected. It all sits in your menu bar and stays out of your way while you work.

  • Pets to name, feed and pet (right-click one)
  • Rain, snow and fog that know where your windows are, or follow the real weather outside
  • Visitors to catch and collect
  • Confetti, fireworks, drums, a Smash Mode and seasonal surprises
  • ⌃⌥⌘P hides everything in a blink

For macOS. No account, no ads, no tracking.
