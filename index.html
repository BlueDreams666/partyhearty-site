Party Hearty

Pets, weather and small toys that live on your Mac's desktop, around your windows.

Your pet naps on the Dock and wanders along the tops of your windows. When it rains, the water runs down the sides of your windows, snow piles up on top of them, and now and then a visitor turns up for you to catch. The whole thing lives in the menu bar and stays out of the way while you work.

Things to play with

  • A pet you can name, feed and pet. Right-click it.
  • Rain, snow and fog that know where your windows are. Or let them follow the actual weather outside.
  • Visitors to catch and keep in a collection.
  • Confetti, fireworks, drums, Smash Mode, and a few surprises depending on the time of year.

When someone walks up behind you, ⌃⌥⌘P hides all of it at once.
